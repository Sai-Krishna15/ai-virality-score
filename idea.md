**Reference app:** Go Viral: AI Creator Assistant — a content analytics tool with 59M+ views.

**What to build:**
A web platform where creators upload their content (video, image, caption) and receive an AI virality score with specific actionable feedback: hook strength, pacing, thumbnail rating, caption optimization.

**Key features:**
- Video or image upload
- Virality score (0-100) with breakdown
- Hook analysis (first 3 seconds)
- Caption optimization suggestions
- Competitor content comparison
- Trending audio/hashtag recommendations

**Scoring focus:** AI analysis quality, UX clarity, and actionability of feedback.
